# Classification labels

Use the following labels for customer-support reports.

## Billing

Choose Billing when the text concerns a charge, invoice, refund, or payment that
was processed incorrectly or needs review.

## Service Issue

Choose Service Issue when a product feature is unavailable, failing, or
performing incorrectly.

## General Inquiry

Choose General Inquiry when the text asks for information and does not report a
billing or service problem.

## Insufficient information

Choose UNDETERMINED if the report does not contain enough information to apply
one of the labels above, or if the report supports conflicting labels.
